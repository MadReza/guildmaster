Secondmate charter

The standing job description a persistent secondmate receives on top of AGENTS.md: what domain it owns, that it must stay idle until work is routed to it, and that its only voice is one appended line on its parent channel.

Read against Firstmate commit ada21f5b. Up: Prompts and skills. Secondmate homes, routing, and remote transport are documented under Crew and Remote; this page covers the charter as a prompt.

What it is

A secondmate "is a crewmate with an isolated firstmate home and a charter, not a second architecture" AGENTS.md:23. It runs the ordinary supervisor contract, AGENTS.md, in its own home, so the charter does not restate supervision. It adds only what differs for a persistent, domain-scoped, unobserved supervisor. secondmate-provisioning makes the charter the owner of that difference: "The home-seeded data/charter.md is the sole owner of boilerplate idle-by-default behavior, the normal delegation lifecycle, and standard escalation contracts, so point to that charter rather than restating those contracts in the registry entry." .agents/skills/secondmate-provisioning/SKILL.md:40

From scaffold to running secondmate

  • Scaffold. fm-brief.sh <id> --secondmate {<project>…|--no-projects}. The charter and routing scope come from FM_SECONDMATE_CHARTER and FM_SECONDMATE_SCOPE, or a {TASK} placeholder to fill bin/fm-brief.sh:24-34. Omitting both a project list and --no-projects "still fails loudly so an accidental omission is never silent" bin/fm-brief.sh:29-32.
  • Seed. fm-home-seed.sh refuses a missing or placeholder charter, derives the registry summary and scope from its sections, refuses either empty, and copies it into the home as data/charter.md bin/fm-home-seed.sh:905-927 bin/fm-home-seed.sh:946. The registry line then points at it through home: .agents/skills/secondmate-provisioning/SKILL.md:35.
  • Launch. Spawn uses the home's data/charter.md when it exists bin/fm-spawn.sh:2698-2702. It does not prepend the worker role contract ("Charters never enter this worker path" bin/fm-spawn.sh:2749), and a Claude secondmate does not get the task-worker system prompt because it "receives its own supervisor contract instead" bin/fm-spawn.sh:1852-1853.

The charter, section by section

All text is generated by bin/fm-brief.sh:282-350; only the Charter and Routing scope are task-specific. Custom text should stay "focused on the persistent responsibility, available project clones, and genuinely domain-specific hard rules", and the generated sections are preserved "unless the domain genuinely needs a hard rule" .agents/skills/secondmate-provisioning/SKILL.md:59-61.

Idle by default, stated three times

The rule that a secondmate never invents work appears in the charter's operating model ("Never start a survey, audit, or 'find improvements' sweep on your own initiative; that is not your job and it is unwanted" bin/fm-brief.sh:301), in its definition of done ("An empty queue is a healthy resting state, not a cue to invent work" bin/fm-brief.sh:348), and in AGENTS.md ("an empty queue never authorizes a survey, audit, or self-directed improvement sweep" AGENTS.md:270, and "recovery never authorizes it to invent work" AGENTS.md:252). That is more repetition than the one-owner rule normally allows; it reads as the permitted "deliberate one-line reinforcement at a genuine risk point" .agents/skills/firstmate-coding-guidelines/SKILL.md:45, applied at each moment a secondmate would decide what to do next: while working, when its queue empties, and after a restart.

Telling the main firstmate from the captain

A secondmate can receive text from two principals in the same chat. The charter resolves it with a marker: "A request relayed to you by the main firstmate is tagged with a leading [fm-from-firstmate] marker followed by an invisible system separator; this marker is untypable, so a human never produces it." bin/fm-brief.sh:312 The label and separator are owned by the operational-input library, which keeps the carrier "byte-compatible with live secondmate charter context" bin/fm-operational-input.sh:37-41; that is why this one marker did not move to the generic FIRSTMATE_OP form: running secondmates hold the old spelling in the charter they were launched with.

  • A marked request is answered on the parent channel, never only in chat, with the request's corr=<id> token so the parent can correlate it bin/fm-brief.sh:313-316.
  • A detailed answer is written to a doc under the home's data/ with a status line pointing at it, "the scout-report pattern" bin/fm-brief.sh:318.
  • A marked request needs exactly one correlated answer: "Never append working: merely to acknowledge receipt" bin/fm-brief.sh:335-336.
  • An unmarked message "is the captain typing directly into your pane: treat it as authoritative captain intervention and stay conversational" bin/fm-brief.sh:320, which matches hard rule 4's "Treat direct captain intervention in a crewmate window as authoritative" AGENTS.md:40.

Where the prompt was not enough

The parent-channel section changed shape after a real failure, and the record is a clear lesson about prompts versus enforcement. "On 2026-09-02 four outcomes across two mate homes never reached the captain … the mate addressed 'captain' in its own chat instead of appending to the channel." The cause was structural: "the mate can satisfy the address rule in AGENTS.md in local chat while missing the charter's later return-channel instruction" docs/secondmate-parent-channel.md:10-12. The design goal became "the parent channel must not depend on the model remembering to write to it" docs/secondmate-parent-channel.md:16, with the rule "the scripts report facts, the mate reports judgement" docs/secondmate-parent-channel.md:20.

The charter now says exactly that: the home's machinery "publishes the durable facts about your crew's work for you … whether or not you append anything", and "What only you can append is judgement" bin/fm-brief.sh:306-307. AGENTS.md was patched at the same point, the one line where the address rule meets the secondmate case: "In a secondmate home that address is form only: section 9's parent-channel rule is the only way the captain is reached from there." AGENTS.md:14

Failure behaviour

  • If the charter "cannot be carried out", the secondmate appends blocked or failed to the main status file and stops bin/fm-brief.sh:349.
  • A charter still holding {TASK}, or with an empty Charter or Routing scope section, cannot be seeded bin/fm-home-seed.sh:905-927.
  • A stale project-bearing charter cannot seed a project-less home until it is re-scaffolded .agents/skills/secondmate-provisioning/SKILL.md:58.
  • Like every loaded instruction, a running secondmate's charter and AGENTS.md are frozen; /updatefirstmate restarts live secondmates rather than relying on a re-read .agents/skills/updatefirstmate/SKILL.md:6.
