Hold buckets

Every captain hold is sorted into exactly one presentation bucket: live, blocked, dated, or aged. Only live holds are waiting on the captain now. They appear on Captain's Call. The other three appear in Charted Next, with the reason they are waiting.

Read against Firstmate commit ada21f5b. Up: Backlog and decisions. The snapshot and Bearings as a whole belong to Observability.

What it is for

A decision list is only useful if every open decision is on it exactly once, and nothing on it is waiting on something other than the captain. Two rules make that true.

  • Structure only, never prose. The bucket is decided "only from structured fields - hold_kind, state, hold_until, unresolved_blocker_ids, and the machine-written hold-set timestamp. Hold reason and body prose are never matched, so no wording can hide, reveal, or reclassify a decision." docs/captain-hold-lifecycle.md:125-126
  • Total and exclusive. "No captain hold can fall through them and none can match two, which is what keeps a hold from vanishing from every view." docs/captain-hold-lifecycle.md:127-128

A regression fixture tests the first rule directly. A hold whose reason says "DEFERRED by captain revisit later", but which carries no --until, stays live, "because only structured fields classify" tests/fm-bearings-snapshot.test.sh:1150-1151 tests/fm-bearings-snapshot.test.sh:1164.

The classifier

Unresolved blocker
A blocked-by id whose structured record is not Done, including an id missing from the backlog: "missing ids stay open" bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:26-27 bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:545-554.
After today
A hold_until string compared with the observation date. A date equal to today is due, matching tasks-axi's "inactive on and after that date" bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:144-151.
Age
hold_age_days = days_between((.hold_set // .since); $now). It counts from the hold-set stamp, falling back to since for older unstamped holds, as "floored elapsed days" bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:535 bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:562 docs/captain-hold-lifecycle.md:127.
Threshold
FM_SNAPSHOT_UNDATED_HOLD_AGE_DAYS, default 14. A value of 0 "ages every hold with a non-negative computed age" bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:207-211 bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:282-285.
Aging is a safety net, not a deferral

"That aging is a projection safety net only. The durable deferral remains re-holding with --until." docs/captain-hold-lifecycle.md:131-132 An aged hold is still an open captain call. open returns 0 for it, and teardown still retains its row. It has only left the default Captain's Call view.

Card hygiene on the board

Before publishing, the board cross-checks every decision card "on exact identity and none on prose". It drops a card in three cases: when open --distinguish-absent returns 1, when the task id or its pr_url appears in the payload's own landed rows, or when a landed row carries the same artifact at an equal or newer version. It keeps a card on exit 2 or 3, "because a card wrongly shown is recoverable and a call wrongly hidden is not". Each dropped card is named on stderr as dropped-landed-card: docs/captain-hold-lifecycle.md:101-115 bin/fm-bearings-board.sh:49-55.

Secondmate holds in the parent's view

A secondmate home publishes a bounded summary. There, "an actionable captain hold [is classified] as captain_decision and preserves every captain hold in the bounded queued inventory of the owning home" docs/captain-hold-lifecycle.md:133. Summaries must declare the current hold-classifier schema, and "older summaries are rejected" bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:262-266. Three limits are accepted deliberately docs/captain-hold-lifecycle.md:141-147:

  • A remote or secondmate hold keeps the producer home's age and aging decision from capture time. The parent does not recompute it.
  • "A rare concurrent answer-close and re-hold race can leave the newly re-held task without its age basis."
  • Summaries are capped by FM_SNAPSHOT_SECONDMATE_DECISIONS and FM_SNAPSHOT_SECONDMATE_QUEUED (default 20 each) bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:162-163. A deferred hold past those caps "is not exported, so it can be neither gated nor revealed".

Open question: non-markdown homes

Not settled by the source

The snapshot computes buckets by parsing the markdown file $DATA/backlog.md directly with jq. When that file is absent it reports present:false with no records bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:134 bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:384-390. It does not call tasks-axi for rows. A home on the Beads adapter keeps no such file, and the source read here does not show how its captain holds reach decisions_open and Captain's Call. It may be through a mechanism outside this area, or not at all.
