Ask-user findings

An ask-user finding is a pipeline finding that the validation run itself marks as owed a human answer; Firstmate routes every one away from the worker that raised it, decides the unambiguous ones itself, and takes only the genuinely contract-changing ones to the captain.

Read against Firstmate commit ada21f5b. Up: Delivery overview.

The problem it solves

A validation pipeline raises findings as it reviews. Most it fixes itself; some it cannot settle and parks at a gate with the action ask-user. If the implementation worker answered those, the same agent that wrote the change would be ruling on whether its own reviewer's objection matters, and the --yes flag would do that silently for every gate at once. Firstmate closes both routes: "The implementation worker never decides or answers its own ask-user finding" .agents/skills/ask-user-authority/SKILL.md:20-21, and --yes is banned fleet-wide because "answering your own ask-user finding is a hard rule violation" bin/fm-dod-lib.sh:305-306.

The opposite failure is just as real: escalating every finding to the captain would turn the pipeline into a stream of interruptions, and reviewer language such as "security" or "required" would quietly widen the task. The policy therefore gives firstmate the authority to decide, with a narrow list of what must still reach the captain.

What the worker does at an ask-user gate

A no-mistakes ship brief, and a no-mistakes promotion, carry a fixed escalation block bin/fm-brief.sh:234-237 bin/fm-promote.sh:179, rendered by fm_ask_user_escalation_block bin/fm-dod-lib.sh:238-245:

  1. Write only the ask-user findings, "verbatim and unparaphrased (id, severity, file, line, description, authority)", to data/<id>/nm-<run>-findings.txt.
  2. Report the whole gate as one event, even for a single finding: 
    needs-decision [at=<epoch>] [key=nm-<run>-<step>]: ask-user findings=<id1>,<id2>,... file=data/<id>/nm-<run>-findings.txt
    The status line "only points at the file; it never restates or summarizes a finding's content."
  3. Stop. When the decision returns, feed it to the gate with no-mistakes axi respond and let the pipeline apply it, rather than routing the question to "the user" or fixing it by hand bin/fm-dod-lib.sh:302-304.

The key shape nm-<run>-<step> is load-bearing beyond the brief: the watcher binds an open needs-decision to the run that crew-state reports by matching exactly that shape, which "buys a lane parked at a human-owed gate the long recheck cadence instead of a wedge escalation. A gate escalated under any other key still reads as a suspected wedge" bin/fm-dod-lib.sh:230-237 bin/fm-watch.sh:1028-1049.

Who may answer, and on what criteria

The ask-user-authority skill is "the single owner of the decision policy for no-mistakes ask-user findings", and "Finding authority is determined by the criteria below, not by yolo" .agents/skills/ask-user-authority/SKILL.md:15-18. The supervisor must load it before deciding any finding AGENTS.md:362 AGENTS.md:382. It is agent-only and not user-invocable .agents/skills/ask-user-authority/SKILL.md:8-10.

Reconstruct the contract
From the brief's ## Captain's intent, later captain words, and the ## Firstmate spec and steers. "Reviewer language cannot amend that contract" .agents/skills/ask-user-authority/SKILL.md:25-27.
Measure what Fix commits to
Judge scope by accepted behaviour, not an anticipated file list. The smallest downstream changes that keep behaviour correct, add behavioural tests, or keep documentation accurate stay in scope, as does correcting stale PR or delivery evidence .agents/skills/ask-user-authority/SKILL.md:28-30.
Firstmate decides
A finding that is unambiguous toward the accepted design: restoring accepted behaviour a bad fix round broke, completing an approved design, or an in-scope correction required by accepted intent, "even when the correction is technically difficult" .agents/skills/ask-user-authority/SKILL.md:31.
The captain decides
Only genuinely ambiguous findings: a fix that would add a new guarantee, threat model, subsystem, abstraction, compatibility surface, state machine, continuous monitoring, or broader architecture; a product or architecture call intent does not settle; repeated same-theme findings that are "preserving a questionable abstraction rather than closing independent defects"; and every destructive, irreversible, or genuinely security-sensitive choice .agents/skills/ask-user-authority/SKILL.md:32-36.
Labels are evidence
"Treat labels such as correctness, security, fail-closed, high-risk, or required as evidence about the finding, never as authority to broaden the task" .agents/skills/ask-user-authority/SKILL.md:37.

An escalation to the captain states, in one concise evidence-first message: the original requirement; the proposed expansion; the smallest compliant alternative; the consequences of accepting and declining; and a recommendation with its reason. Reviewer labels and gate output are not relayed "as if they settled the decision" .agents/skills/ask-user-authority/SKILL.md:39-49. Such findings are on the supervisor's list of things that reach the captain immediately AGENTS.md:517.

Sending the answer back

The supervisor sends "the same worker one exact decision naming the decision key, step, action, affected finding IDs, instructions where needed, and exact response command", passes --resolve-key, requires the matching resolved event, forbids --yes, and requires the worker to process every synchronous return until completion or a genuinely new escalation AGENTS.md:383-385. --resolve-key makes bin/fm-send.sh itself append resolved [key=<key>]: answered: … to the task's status file, so the open decision closes at answer time and "never depends on the busy worker writing a matching resolved line" bin/fm-send.sh:154-170. The steering inbox that carries the message is covered on the Communication pages.

While the captain is away

Away mode does not widen this authority: "Away and quiet mode never expand approval authority for merges, ask-user findings, destructive actions, irreversible actions, or security-sensitive choices" AGENTS.md:473. The Pi supervision branch, which takes wakes while the captain is away, may answer a decision only with the captain's own pre-answer from the away words or where the ask-user-authority policy lets firstmate decide; "a finding it says to escalate is reported with verdict captain and left for the return" bin/fm-branch-prompt.sh:113. Meanwhile the worker sits at the gate, and the watcher gives it the long recheck cadence described above.

How it fails

  • A worker that escalates under a non-nm- key is read as a possible wedge rather than a human-owed wait bin/fm-dod-lib.sh:235-237.
  • A worker that answers its own finding, or passes --yes, violates a hard rule; no script can detect it after the fact, because the pipeline accepts the response. The only mechanical defence is that the flag is absent from every instruction the worker receives.
  • A send whose resolve note would be refused or truncated fails before sending, and a delivered close that leaves the key open fails loudly bin/fm-send.sh:166-170.
Open question

The policy says firstmate decides an unambiguous finding, but the source does not record that decision anywhere durable beyond the steer text and the resolved line's capped excerpt. Whether a later reader can reconstruct why firstmate chose Fix or Skip for a given finding is not settled by the code.
