Scout reports

A scout task's only product is data/<id>/report.md: a standalone document that lives in the home's private records, is required before teardown, and stays readable after the scout's workspace and task state are gone.

Why the report, not the workspace, is the deliverable

A scout "produces knowledge in data/<id>/report.md, never a PR" (AGENTS.md:308). Its worktree is scratch: the scout brief states "the deliverable is a report at data/<task-id>/report.md (no branch, no push, no PR) and the worktree is scratch" (bin/fm-brief.sh:20-21). The home layout records the difference in lifetime: <id>/report.md is the "scout task deliverable, written by the crewmate; survives teardown" (AGENTS.md:100), while state/ is removed with the task. Knowledge routing sends investigation findings to exactly this place: "investigation findings belong in the scout report" (AGENTS.md:278).

What the scout is told to write

The scout brief's definition of done: "Write your findings to $DATA/$ID/report.md. The report must stand alone: what you did, what you found, the evidence (commands run, output, file:line references), and what you recommend" (bin/fm-brief.sh:463-464). Before reporting done it must follow the captain-hold lifecycle and "pass its shared completion gate for the report and any visual review", then append done [at=<epoch>]: {one-line conclusion} (bin/fm-brief.sh:466-467). If the findings show work that should ship, the scout says so in the report, and firstmate may promote the task in place (bin/fm-brief.sh:468).

When the Lavish review loop is available the brief also offers it; otherwise it asks for a text report (bin/fm-brief.sh:22-23).

The completion gate

A report is where unresolved captain questions tend to be discovered, so it cannot be treated as complete while one is untracked. "Every unresolved question that belongs to the captain and is discovered while producing, reading, presenting, or ending an investigation or visual review must be carried by a captain-held task ... before that work or review may be treated as complete" (.agents/skills/captain-hold-lifecycle/SKILL.md:19). The scripts never read the report to find those questions: "scripts must not infer captain calls from report prose" (.agents/skills/captain-hold-lifecycle/SKILL.md:15). The agent inventories the report, then runs bin/fm-captain-hold.sh complete <origin> with every held task id, or --none (.agents/skills/captain-hold-lifecycle/SKILL.md:23). Finishing or archiving the report never closes a held task (.agents/skills/captain-hold-lifecycle/SKILL.md:26). The lifecycle is documented under Backlog and decisions.

Teardown refuses without it

For an ordinary ship task, teardown refuses unless the work has landed. Scouts are carved out: "their worktree is declared scratch and the report at data/<task-id>/report.md is the work product. Teardown proceeds only once the report exists and the shared unresolved-decision completion gate verifies its captain-held inventory" (bin/fm-teardown.sh:72-75). The refusals are explicit (bin/fm-teardown.sh:3280-3292):

REFUSED: scout task <id> has no report at <path>.
The report is the work product. Have the crewmate write it, or use --force after explicit discard approval.

REFUSED: scout task <id> has not passed the captain-call completion gate.

On success the backlog's Done transition carries --report <data-relative>/<id>/report.md, so the closed row points at the deliverable (bin/fm-teardown.sh:1520-1528). Teardown mechanics belong to Delivery.

How reports are found later

bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh lists every regular file matching data/*/report.md, two levels deep, sorted by id, as scout_reports[] with {id, path} (bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:1957-1970, bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:71). Each entry is tagged with a kind taken from the live task or backlog row, defaulting to scout when neither exists (bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:2059, bin/fm-fleet-snapshot.sh:2069). Discovery is by path, not by task record, which is what lets a report be found after its task's meta is deleted.

Bearings shows only the reports of live and recently landed tasks by default, with --all-reports for the full inventory (bin/fm-bearings-snapshot.sh:450, bin/fm-bearings-snapshot.sh:578-581). The digest places report pointers under Underway as "pickup pointers worth reopening", and a completed scout's report pointer never belongs in Captain's Call (.agents/skills/bearings/SKILL.md:89, .agents/skills/bearings/SKILL.md:171). See Bearings.

Open question: retention

The source defines no retention or pruning for data/<id>/report.md. The only related statements are that data/ as a whole is local and gitignored (AGENTS.md:92) and that reports survive teardown. Whether reports are ever cleaned up, and by whom, is not settled by the code read for this page.

The same pattern elsewhere

  • A secondmate is told to put a detailed answer "in a doc under your home's data/ and append a status line that points to that doc - the scout-report pattern" (bin/fm-brief.sh:318).
  • Promotion turns a scout into a ship task without losing its context: bin/fm-promote.sh flips kind= to ship, requires --mode and --yolo, and firstmate decides those "having just read the scout's report" (bin/fm-promote.sh:2-25). The report stays where it was.
