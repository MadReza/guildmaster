Kinds of worker

Firstmate commands three kinds of direct report: the ephemeral ship crewmate that changes a project, the scout whose deliverable is knowledge, and the persistent secondmate that is a whole Firstmate of its own. They share one mechanism and differ in records, gates, and behaviour.

Read against Firstmate commit ada21f5b. Up: Crew.

One mechanism, three kinds

The supervisor contract is explicit that there is no separate architecture for the persistent kind: "A secondmate is a crewmate with an isolated firstmate home and a charter, not a second architecture" AGENTS.md:23. All three are created by bin/fm-spawn.sh, recorded in state/<id>.meta of the home that spawned them, reached through the same backend adapter, and retired by bin/fm-teardown.sh. The script's usage line shows the three shapes bin/fm-spawn.sh:2-6:

fm-spawn.sh <task-id> <project-dir> --mode <no-mistakes|direct-PR|local-only> --yolo <on|off> …
fm-spawn.sh <task-id> <project-dir> --scout …
fm-spawn.sh <task-id> [<firstmate-home>] … --secondmate

The kind is written once as kind=ship|scout|secondmate bin/fm-spawn.sh:196-198, and changes only through promotion, which flips a scout to a ship.

Ship crewmate: the ephemeral task worker

Purpose. Ship is the default kind and "produces a project change through the selected delivery mode" AGENTS.md:307. It is dispatched for exactly one task and torn down once that task's work has landed.

  • Delivery contract is mandatory. --mode and --yolo are "REQUIRED for every ship spawn and refused on --scout and --secondmate spawns" bin/fm-spawn.sh:7-9. The implementation refuses a missing flag, the registry-only value no-mistakes-prod-only, and any other value bin/fm-spawn.sh:754-782.
  • Brief and record must agree. The ship brief carries a fixed line Delivery contract: mode=<mode>. A spawn passing a different --mode refuses, and a brief with no such line only warns bin/fm-spawn.sh:2785-2795.
  • Standing posture is advisory. A mode with less rigor than the project's registered posture prints a loud notice and proceeds bin/fm-spawn.sh:2796-2804.
  • Records. kind=ship, mode=, yolo=, plus the common fields below. pr= and pr_head= are added later by bin/fm-pr-check.sh when the worker reports a ready PR bin/fm-pr-check.sh:2-3.
  • Teardown gate. Uncommitted changes refuse. Unpushed commits refuse unless the landed-work test proves them merged. local-only work must be merged into the local default branch bin/fm-teardown.sh:1780-1843.

Scout: the worker whose deliverable is knowledge

Purpose. A scout "produces knowledge in data/<id>/report.md, never a PR". It suits investigation, diagnosis, planning, reproduction, or audit work, and only when the captain asks for a separate knowledge deliverable or when uncertainty "could materially change whether or what to build" AGENTS.md:308.

  • No delivery posture. The spawn refuses --mode and --yolo for a scout bin/fm-spawn.sh:783-793, and the record carries neither bin/fm-spawn.sh:4402-4405. Teardown treats an absent mode as no-mistakes, and promotion requires an explicit mode bin/fm-spawn.sh:4392-4397.
  • A scratch laboratory. The scout brief says "The worktree is your laboratory - install, run, edit, and make scratch commits freely; all of it is discarded at teardown", and rule 1 is "Never push to any remote and never open a PR" bin/fm-brief.sh:419-423.
  • The report is the product. It must "stand alone: what you did, what you found, the evidence …, and what you recommend", and the scout must pass the captain-hold-lifecycle completion gate before reporting done bin/fm-brief.sh:463-467.
  • Teardown gate. Teardown skips the dirty and landed checks for a scout. It refuses instead when data/<id>/report.md is missing or fm-captain-hold.sh verify fails bin/fm-teardown.sh:3280-3292. The report lives under data/, so it survives the worktree.
  • A report is not authorization. "A report may recommend implementation but does not authorize it", and a separately authorized fix promotes the same scout rather than spawning a duplicate AGENTS.md:411-415. See Scout promotion.

Secondmate: the persistent worker with its own home

Purpose. A secondmate owns a domain. "Each secondmate has a persistent isolated FM_HOME, including its own state, backlog, projects, and session lock" AGENTS.md:55. It "is idle by default and acts only on work routed by the main firstmate" AGENTS.md:269, and "an empty queue is healthy" AGENTS.md:406. Inside its home it runs the same supervisor contract and spawns its own crewmates.

How its spawn differs

  • The home is validated, not created. validate_firstmate_home_for_spawn refuses a home that is the filesystem root, the active home, or the Firstmate repo, or that sits inside or above either of them. It also refuses a home without the .fm-secondmate-home marker naming this exact id, AGENTS.md, or bin/ bin/fm-spawn.sh:2539-2591. Homes are provisioned beforehand by bin/fm-home-seed.sh, often on a durable Treehouse lease (details).
  • The home is brought up to date. Before launch the home is fast-forwarded to the primary checkout's default-branch commit when that is safe. A dirty, diverged, or wrong-branch home launches unchanged with a warning bin/fm-spawn.sh:2651-2675. Primary-authoritative local configuration is then propagated into it bin/fm-spawn.sh:2677-2696.
  • Its instructions are a charter. The launch reads <home>/data/charter.md, falling back to data/<id>/brief.md in the primary bin/fm-spawn.sh:2698-2702. It never receives the ship and scout worker role contract, and on Claude it never receives the task-worker --append-system-prompt statement bin/fm-spawn.sh:1852-1858. See Launch.
  • It becomes a Firstmate. The launch line clears the primary's path overrides and sets FM_HOME to the secondmate home, plus a supervision model: autoarm for claude and cursor, extension for pi, pi-signed and omp, and persistent for the rest bin/fm-spawn.sh:4655-4672.
  • Harness and backend limits. muse, gemini, agy and rovo are refused because none has a primary supervision protocol bin/fm-spawn.sh:2109-2134. The orca and cmux backends refuse --secondmate bin/fm-spawn.sh:1533-1540.
  • It can live on another host. A remotely routed secondmate takes spawn_remote_secondmate first. That path records window=remote:<id> and prints a success line with remote= and backend= bin/fm-spawn.sh:1510-1516 bin/fm-spawn.sh:1076. The Remote area owns that path.
  • It is never a work item. The backlog gate skips secondmates entirely bin/fm-backlog-transition-lib.sh:283-286, and "A persistent secondmate is recorded in the secondmate registry and runtime state, never as a backlog work item" AGENTS.md:333.

How it behaves differently afterwards

  • Current state. An idle pane is its healthy state, so fm-crew-state.sh skips the busy check for it and reads its status log only bin/fm-crew-state.sh:1203-1215.
  • Messages. Text sent to it always carries the from-firstmate routing marker, because its reply must come back through the status path docs/agent-control.md:5-6.
  • Relaunch. A secondmate relaunch re-resolves its durable config/secondmate-harness pin, needs no --note, and never rewrites its charter. A secondmate whose endpoint is gone is respawned by fm-spawn.sh --secondmate from the session-start liveness sweep, not by relaunch docs/agent-control.md:58-79 docs/agent-control.md:117 AGENTS.md:191.
  • Retirement. It is retired only on an explicit decision AGENTS.md:407. Normal teardown refuses while its home has in-flight task records bin/fm-teardown.sh:3264, and --force is the approved discard path for its children bin/fm-teardown.sh:147-163.

Crewmates never address the captain

All three kinds report only to the Firstmate that spawned them: "All crewmate communication flows through firstmate" AGENTS.md:38-40. A secondmate reaches the captain only through its parent's channel.
