Containers

The separately running services and separately kept stores inside one Honeycomb region, what each is responsible for, and how they talk. Honeycomb's internals are proprietary: everything here comes from its engineers' own posts, talks, incident reports and book, and anything this site had to infer is drawn in amber.

Read on 2026-10-02. Up: System context. Down: Retriever, Refinery.

The shape in one paragraph

Telemetry arrives at a thin, stateless ingest service, which writes it to one Kafka topic talk/blog shipit-11 confluent-podcast. Kafka is what lets the stateless ingest workers be restarted independently of the stateful storage engine, and it holds enough history that a storage bug can be fixed by replaying blog-scaling-kafka. Each partition is consumed by a pair of Retriever nodes that write column files to local disk and age them to S3; a query fans out across every Retriever holding the dataset and, for older data, across thousands of short Lambda invocations, then merges blog-kafka-may-2026 qcon-2022. Everything else, SLOs, the Service Map and anomaly baselines, is another reader of the same stream blog-kafka-may-2026. There is no pre-aggregation anywhere on the write path: "we only read at query time, we don't do any aggregation or indexing, even calculated columns are all done at query time" infoq-podcast-kerr.

What every container relies on

Three copies of the whole picture talk/blog
"We run three environments: production for our customers' telemetry, dogfood for analyzing production's telemetry, and kibble for observing dogfood in turn" blog-m6g. Production's own telemetry reaches dogfood through an internal Refinery cluster blog-graviton4, and there is one Kafka cluster per environment in each of the two regions, six in all blog-kafka-2026.
Small, frequent, gated deploys talk/blog
A cron job deploys "like every ten minutes", first to kibble, then dogfood, then production shipit-11; production follows dogfood by about 20 minutes and stops automatically "if our SLOs don't match, if we get too many errors in dog food" qcon-2022.
Failure is rehearsed talk/blog
One Retriever node and one Kafka broker per cluster are terminated every week blog-scaling-kafka. A replacement Retriever restores its predecessor's hourly snapshot and Kafka offset and replays at most two hours blog-scaling-kafka. In 2024 Honeycomb "abruptly destroyed one third of the infrastructure in our production environment" to prove it survives losing a zone blog-chaos-1.
Best effort by design talk/blog
"Honeycomb is designed as a best-effort querying system which is ok with occasional incorrect answers" blog-slo-challenges; percentiles are t-digest estimates and COUNT_DISTINCT a HyperLogLog estimate documented docs-query-build.
One codebase, two deployments documented
Private Cloud "uses the same architecture and codebase as Honeycomb SaaS" docs-private-cloud, and its requirement to run Kafka on EKS was one reason for the 2026 Kafka migration blog-kafka-2026.
