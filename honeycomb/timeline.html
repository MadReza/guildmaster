How it is done

Timeline, 2016 to 2026

How the architecture, the product and the company changed, in one sequence. The architecture lane is the story of the engineering decisions: a disk-based column store, then S3 and Lambda, then ARM, then a stream processor for SLOs, then Kubernetes, Private Cloud and open-source Kafka.

Read on 2026-10-02. Dates are each source's own; a month means the source dates the event no closer.

Four eras

2016–2018: a Scuba for everyone else
Retriever on local disk, Kafka in front, Go everywhere; "no indices to maintain on the write path" yen-under-the-hood. Older data moved to S3 in 2018, but slowly: secondary queries took minutes blog-secondary-storage.
2019–2021: make it cheap enough to keep
Lambda over S3 segments made old data fast blog-secondary-storage; event-based pricing replaced storage-based pricing pr-pricing-2020; Graviton and tiered Kafka cut costs by large fractions blog-graviton-1yr blog-scaling-kafka; Beagle moved SLOs off repeated queries blog-beagle.
2022–2024: an enterprise platform
Environments, the Service Map, Kubernetes, the EU region and virtual datasets for very large environments changelog-es blog-service-map blog-k8s-migration pr-eu blog-virtual-datasets; Gartner moved Honeycomb from Leader (2022, 2023) to Visionary (2024 onward) pr-gartner-2022 blog-gartner-2026.
2025–2026: agents on both sides
AI agents as users, through MCP and Canvas, and as the thing observed, through GenAI conventions and Agent Timeline pr-hi pr-agent-obs; Private Cloud and open-source Kafka underneath docs-private-cloud blog-kafka-2026; and a Pro price that more than doubled per event docs-pro-2026.
